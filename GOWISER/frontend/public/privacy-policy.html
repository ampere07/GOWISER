Location Data (Including Background Location)

The GOWISER mobile app collects precise location data. The details below explain exactly what is collected, when, and why.

Who this applies to

Continuous location collection applies to GO WISER technician and field-staff accounts only. Customer and office accounts are never continuously tracked; the app asks them for a location only when they actively tap an option that needs one (for example, dropping a pin on a service address).

What is collected

  • Precise location: latitude and longitude
  • Accuracy, speed, and heading reported by the device
  • The date and time of each reading

When it is collected — including in the background

  • For a signed-in technician on duty, location is collected and sent to GO WISER approximately every 10 seconds.
  • This collection continues in the background — when the app is minimised, when the screen is off, and after the app has been swiped away from recent apps.
  • On Android, a persistent notification ("GOWISER location sharing active") is shown for the whole time background location sharing is running, so it is always visible to you. On iOS the system location indicator is shown.
  • Collection stops as soon as you sign out of the app, or when you withdraw the permission in your device settings.

Why we collect it

  • To show your position on the dispatch team's live monitoring map while you are on duty
  • To assign job orders to the nearest available technician and estimate arrival times
  • To confirm that a site visit, installation, or repair took place at the customer's address
  • To record accurate coordinates for network equipment (LCP/NAP) and customer addresses
  • For workforce safety, timekeeping, and dispute resolution on field work

Location data is used only for the purposes above. We do not sell it, and we do not use or share it for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

Your control over location

  • The app shows a clear disclosure explaining background collection before your device ever asks for location permission.
  • Granting location is your choice. If you choose "Only while using the app" instead of "Allow all the time", sharing happens only while the app is open on screen — everything else in the app keeps working.
  • If you deny location entirely, the app still works; only location-dependent features (live map position, nearest-technician assignment, pin capture) are unavailable.
  • You may withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings, or by signing out.

Note for employees: location sharing while on duty is part of GO WISER's field-operations process. Withdrawing permission may prevent job orders from being assigned or verified, but it never affects the security of your device or your other app data.
